# Community Edition

Kaxu follows an open-core model.

The Community Edition is intended to provide:

- `SessionOperation` and deterministic state transitions;
- versioned command and event schemas;
- the Agent Adapter SDK and community adapters;
- a local Host and session boundary;
- a responsive Web/PWA Projection;
- self-hosting primitives;
- public architecture and integration documentation.

Managed relay, team governance, enterprise security, compliance integrations, hosted operations, and support may be provided separately.

The public protocol must remain usable by the Community Edition without a commercial account. Commercial implementations may extend the system through the same public ports and contracts, but may not change the meaning of public operations for self-hosted users.
